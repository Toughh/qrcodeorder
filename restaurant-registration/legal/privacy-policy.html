Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

QR Order SaaS respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you register for or use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide when registering or using the platform, including:

  • Restaurant or business name;
  • Owner or authorized user details;
  • Email address and contact information;
  • Account login information;
  • Restaurant settings and branch information;
  • Menu and business content uploaded to the platform;
  • Order and service-related information;
  • Payment and subscription-related information where applicable.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage your restaurant account;
  • Provide QR ordering and restaurant management services;
  • Generate and manage QR codes and digital menus;
  • Process subscriptions and applicable payments;
  • Provide customer support;
  • Improve platform functionality and security;
  • Communicate important service, billing, or account updates;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Payments

Where payments are required after a free trial or for paid subscriptions, payment processing may be handled by authorized third-party payment providers.

Payment providers may process your payment information according to their own privacy policies and terms. QR Order SaaS does not intentionally store complete card details unless explicitly required and supported through an authorized payment solution.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared only where necessary to operate the platform, provide requested services, process payments, comply with legal requirements, or protect the security and integrity of our services.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain your account, meet legal and accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Cookies and Technical Information

The platform may collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, and usage data to help operate, secure, and improve the service.

8. Your Responsibilities

Restaurant account owners are responsible for ensuring that information and content uploaded to the platform is lawful and that they have appropriate rights and permissions to use such information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the support or contact information available through the QR Order SaaS platform.
